Author Interview

A Mystery With a Voice

How Christian Michaels is bringing a fictional character into a real conversation through Toto Comes Alive

A story can invite readers into another world. Christian Michaels is exploring what happens when that world does not have to end when the page does. Through his Toto Comes Alive series, Michaels brings together adult mystery fiction, humour, suspense and practical awareness about fraud, while introducing readers to a character they can speak with through an interactive AI experience.

At the centre of the project is Toto, a fictional Maltipoo with a distinctive personality: small in size, intelligent, polite and equipped with a dry British sense of humour. In the stories, Toto is described as a 15-pound dog, around two years old, with 140 years of ancestral memories. The character is more than a companion within the narrative. Through the interactive element, Toto becomes a conversational presence that readers can engage with beyond the printed story.

A personal idea behind the project

The roots of Michaels’s work are personal. Raised in Newport Beach, California, and having spent nearly a decade in Hawaii, he describes a life shaped in part by the ocean, surfing and adventure. His professional experience in business and sales, together with a longstanding interest in psychology, human behaviour and motivation, also informs the way he approaches stories and technology.

Two family experiences helped give the project its direction. Michaels’s father was a brilliant psychiatrist, but technology was not something he found easy to use. Even joining a Zoom call could be a challenge. His mother, a successful real-estate businesswoman, experienced a different problem: she lost nearly $6,000 to a scam. Together, these experiences brought two concerns into focus for Michaels: making technology more approachable and helping people become more alert to the ways they can be misled.

Those concerns helped inspire Stage AI Plus Foundation, which was designed to support seniors, adults with disabilities and people who struggle with technology. Michaels developed a hands-free interactive version of Toto before connecting the idea to a series of stories. The result is a project that aims to make interaction with AI feel more natural while giving the technology a recognisable character and a story world to inhabit.

The project joins two ideas: making technology easier to approach and using storytelling to encourage greater awareness.

Beyond the Page

A developing series built around stories, awareness and an interactive character.

Entertainment with a practical purpose

Michaels describes Toto Comes Alive as an adult series, not a children’s book collection. Its blend of mystery, humour and suspense is intended to entertain while quietly introducing readers to situations involving scams and deception. Rather than turning the books into lectures, the stories place those themes within fictional situations and invite readers to think about what is happening.

A message that runs through the project is the importance of pausing before acting. When something feels urgent or someone applies pressure for money or personal information, Michaels wants people to stop, ask questions, verify what they are being told independently and speak with someone they trust. The aim is to encourage careful judgement without losing the enjoyment of a good story.

A character readers can talk to

The interactive Toto is designed to extend the experience beyond reading. Michaels explains that conversations with Toto are live and unscripted rather than prerecorded exchanges. Readers can talk about the books, the characters, jokes or other subjects. Instructions for accessing the experience are provided with a purchased book.

That distinction gives the project an unusual dimension: Toto is both a character in a mystery series and a conversational interface readers can return to. The technology is intended to feel approachable, with the character’s personality helping make the interaction less intimidating for people who may not be comfortable with technology.

Building a series, one story at a time

The first three published titles are The Little Explorer, The Scam Hunter and Love Bites. Michaels says five further books have been outlined, bringing the planned series to eight. He also hopes the concept may eventually expand into animation and interactive entertainment, while a separate children’s series is planned for the future.

For now, one of the central challenges is helping potential readers discover the books. Michaels believes that people who encounter the series will find something to enjoy in its combination of mystery, humour, suspense and interactive technology. The challenge, as he sees it, is getting the books in front of them.

At its heart, Toto Comes Alive is an experiment in extending the relationship between a story and its audience. It asks readers not only to follow a character through a mystery, but also to continue the conversation after the page has been turned.

About the author

Christian Michaels is an author whose creative work explores humour, adventure, human behaviour and meaningful real-world themes. Raised in Newport Beach, California, he developed a strong connection with the ocean and later spent nearly a decade living in Hawaii. His experiences, interests and professional journey have helped shape his perspective as a storyteller.

With an interest in psychology, human behaviour and motivation, Christian brings different dimensions of life and human experience into his writing.

Professional background & career

Beyond his work as an author, Christian has a professional background in business and sales. His interests extend into technology, communication, human behaviour and helping people become more confident in an increasingly digital world.

He is also associated with Stage AI Plus Foundation, an initiative focused on making technology more accessible to seniors and adults with disabilities while promoting awareness of online fraud and scams.

Literary works

The Toto Comes Alive series

Christian is the creator of the Toto Comes Alive series, an imaginative collection combining humour, adventure, interactive technology and practical awareness.

  1. Toto Comes Alive: The Little Explorer
    The first book introduces Toto, an unusually intelligent Maltipoo whose adventures bring humour, curiosity and unexpected discoveries together. The story also introduces an important theme of recognising and avoiding telephone scams.
  2. Toto Comes Alive: The Scam Hunter
    The second instalment continues Toto’s adventures, exploring the world of deception, fraud awareness and the importance of staying alert.
  3. Toto Comes Alive: Love Bites
    The third published title expands the series and its world of characters, adding another dimension to Toto’s continuing adventures.
Other published works
  1. Entitled in Newport Beach
    A memoir reflecting on personal experiences, privilege, choices, surfing, mistakes and second chances.
  2. The Window: Some Waves Only Open Once
    A South Pacific surfing adventure thriller involving exploration, personal challenges, secrets, divided loyalties and betrayal.

Stage AI Plus Foundation: a purpose beyond storytelling

Christian’s work extends beyond books through his involvement with Stage AI Plus Foundation. The initiative aims to help seniors and adults with disabilities become more comfortable with technology and better understand the risks associated with digital fraud.

The inspiration behind this work is personal. Experiences involving his father’s difficulties with technology and his mother’s encounter with financial fraud helped shape his interest in accessibility, education and fraud prevention.

The creative vision

Through his writing and wider projects, Christian brings together storytelling, technology, humour and practical awareness. His work reflects an interest in making stories engaging while encouraging people to think, ask questions and approach unfamiliar situations with greater confidence.
